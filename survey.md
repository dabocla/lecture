# Student Surveys

Keep both short (2–3 min). They're anonymous. Use BrightSpace or Google Forms.

## Pre-Lecture (send ~1 week before)

1. **Major:** Psychology / Nursing / Computer Science / Biology / Agriculture / Other
2. **How often do you use AI tools?** Never / Monthly / Weekly / Daily
3. **What do you mostly use it for?** (check all that apply) Homework help / Writing / Studying / Coding / Research / Personal / Don't use it
4. **How worried are you that AI will affect your future job?** 1 (not at all) – 5 (very)
5. **How confident are you using AI well?** 1 – 5
6. **Do you think using AI for school is cheating?** Always / Depends / Rarely / Never
7. **What's one question about AI you want answered on Dec 3?** (open text)

> Q7 gives you your Q&A material in advance. Q4 makes a good stat to show on the opening slide.

## Post-Lecture (QR code at the end)

1. **How worried are you now that AI will affect your job?** 1 – 5 *(compare to the pre-survey)*
2. **How confident are you using AI now?** 1 – 5
3. **What's one way you'll use AI in your field?** (open text)
4. **What was most useful?** (open text)
5. **Would you take a full course on AI in your field?** Yes / Maybe / No

> The before/after change in Q1–Q2 and the interest in Q5 are useful evidence for the UCM AI board.
