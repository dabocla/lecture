Four myths. True or false?

MYTH 1

“AI is going to take my job.”

AI takes over tasks, not whole jobs. People who can direct AI will replace people who can’t.

MYTH 2

“Using AI is cheating.”

Only if it replaces your thinking. Employers expect you to use it.

MYTH 3

“It’s just a Google you talk to.”

That’s the smallest thing it does. It also drafts, critiques, tutors, analyzes data and takes actions.

MYTH 4

“Companies must build their own AI.”

Most use existing models, connect them to their own data and add guardrails.
