## Perceptions to address
- **Professors:** AI = a way for students to cheat.
- **Students:** afraid AI will take their jobs.
- **General:** "Prompting" is seen as just a Google you talk to — the minimum of what AI can do.
- **Confusion about LLMs:** some corporations think they need to build their own LLM for safety. Reality: most need to *use* existing models well — connected to their own data, with the right guardrails.

## What employers actually want
Leverage AI to:
- Improve efficiency
- Reduce errors
- Increase productivity
- Increase creativity
